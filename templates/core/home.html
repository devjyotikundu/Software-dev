How it works

Four steps from signing up to practising with a real person.

  1. Check your level

    Answer short practice questions. Your results give an estimated proficiency, not a certificate.

  2. Meet a compatible partner

    See people whose languages, goals and schedule fit yours, and why each one is a good match.

  3. Practise together

    Chat in a private room and take turns, so each of you gets time in the language you are learning.

  4. Get better matches

    Your practice results and session feedback shape who we suggest next.
